## 4. Test TC-04: Atomic Transaction & Holdings Consistency Test

### Step A: Execute Buy Trade (20 AAPL @ $225.00)
- **Previous Holding**: Quantity `50.0`, Average Buy Price `$210.00`.
- **Transaction**: BUY `20.0` AAPL @ `$225.00`.
- **Expected Holding**:
  - Quantity: $50 + 20 = 70.0$
  - Average Buy Price: $\frac{(50 \times 210) + (20 \times 225)}{70} = \frac{10,500 + 4,500}{70} = \$214.285714$
- **Actual Holding Query Result**:
  - `quantity`: `70.00000000`
  - `average_buy_price`: `214.285714`
  - **Status: PASSED**

### Step B: Execute Sell Trade (10 NVDA @ $126.00)
- **Previous Holding**: Quantity `80.0`, Average Buy Price `$105.50`.
- **Transaction**: SELL `10.0` NVDA @ `$126.00`.
- **Expected Holding**:
  - Quantity: $80 - 10 = 70.0$
  - Average Buy Price: Remains `$105.50` (unrealized cost basis is not altered by partial exits).
- **Actual Holding Query Result**:
  - `quantity`: `70.00000000`
  - `average_buy_price`: `105.500000`
  - **Status: PASSED**

### Step C: Audit Log Auto-Generation
- Querying `audit_logs` confirmed two new log entries generated synchronously by `trg_log_transaction_activity`:
  - `TRADE_EXECUTION: BUY 20.00000000 AAPL @ 225.000000`
  - `TRADE_EXECUTION: SELL 10.00000000 NVDA @ 126.000000`
  - **Status: PASSED**
